ABOUT AGRITECH

Helping farmers grow a better future.

AgriTech combines accessible design with practical agricultural intelligence.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make farming knowledge simple, fast, and accessible on any device. From everyday smartphones to desktop displays, AgriTech delivers crop cultivation guides, AI leaf diagnostics, live weather updates, and daily commodity prices.

📋 Core Features

  • ✓ Installable mobile-ready web application with dark mode support.
  • ✓ Searchable All-India crop directory synced with daily market commodities.
  • ✓ Camera and gallery-based plant leaf scanning and AI disease detection.
  • ✓ Real-time city weather forecasts and daily Mandi market rates.
